# 👋 Hi there! I'm Diego

I'm a **Software Engineer**, **Java Full-Stack Developer**, and digital entrepreneur with more than four years of professional experience building web applications and enterprise software.

I currently work as a **Java Consultant at [SI2001](https://www.si2001.it/)** while building digital products and businesses independently.

I'm the founder of **[Hextern Digital Studio](https://hexternds.com/)**, where I help businesses build websites, e-commerce platforms, custom software, AI integrations, automations, and scalable digital solutions.

I'm also the founder and developer of **[Prenvia.me](https://prenvia.me/)**, a SaaS platform designed to simplify online booking and appointment management for service-based businesses.
